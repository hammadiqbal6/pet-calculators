# AI SEO / GEO audit

Governing documents: `docs/SEO-PLAYBOOK.md` section 4 and top-10 items 4 and 5; `docs/SEO-GUIDE.md` section 9. Google's position (May 2026 guide, cited in the playbook): optimizing for generative AI search "is still SEO". There is **no special markup**, and llms.txt is ignored by Google Search. Do not sell, or accept, tactics beyond what the playbook supports.

## 1. What to verify

### A. Crawl access (highest impact, cheapest)

1. Built `robots.txt` (generated by `src/pages/robots.txt.ts`): `npm run build && cat dist/robots.txt`. Confirm groups for `Googlebot`, `Bingbot`, `OAI-SearchBot`, `ChatGPT-User`, `PerplexityBot`, `Claude-SearchBot` are `Allow: /`, and a `Sitemap:` line with the production host. `npm run audit:seo` enforces the blocking rules (`robots-block-bot`, `robots-assets`, `robots-sitemap`).
2. Training bots (`GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `Google-Extended`, `CCBot`, `Bytespider`) are a **business decision**, currently left allowed (commented opt-out in `robots.txt.ts`). Blocking them does not affect Google Search (playbook section 4). Record the current decision in the report.
3. Live check at the CDN (docs/DEPLOYMENT.md section 6): `curl -s https://<host>/robots.txt`; Cloudflare Security > Events for blocked bot requests; Bot Fight Mode / "Block AI bots" / managed robots.txt not overriding the file; WAF allow-rules for PerplexityBot IPs per Perplexity docs.
4. `X-Robots-Tag` and meta robots: no `noindex`, `nosnippet`, `max-snippet:0` on content pages (these remove pages from AI features). `BaseLayout.astro` emits `index, follow, max-image-preview:large`; embeds are `noindex, follow` on purpose.
5. Content in initial HTML: `curl -s <url> | rg "<h1|answer-first|<h2"`. The tool UI needs JS, but H1, answer, formula, worked example, FAQ and sources must not.

### B. Answer-first, citation-ready content (per page)

For each tool/guide, check:
- [ ] The first paragraph (`class="answer-first"` or the guide `lead`) states the direct answer/formula in 1-2 sentences with concrete numbers.
- [ ] A worked example with real numbers appears under its own H2 and matches what the calculator outputs for the same inputs (AI answers quote these; mismatches get quoted too).
- [ ] Rates, ranges and thresholds are in **tables** with a unit pair (kg/lb) and a source and "Last updated".
- [ ] Headings are specific questions or nouns ("How many calories does a 20 kg dog need?"), not "Overview".
- [ ] Each page has a unique point of view or dataset (breed table, size-aware conversion, calorie factors) so it is not commodity text.
- [ ] Assumptions and limits are explicit (when the tool is wrong), plus "see a veterinarian" triggers.
- [ ] FAQ answers are complete sentences that stand alone (they are extracted out of context).

### C. Entity and trust signals

- [ ] One brand name (`siteConfig.siteName`) used consistently in title suffix, Organization JSON-LD, About, author box.
- [ ] `Organization` + `WebSite` JSON-LD present (layout); `sameAs` populated only with real profiles.
- [ ] `/about/`, `/authors/`, `/methodology/` name real people and the evidence base; reviewer credentials are real or absent (no placeholders, no invented credentials).
- [ ] Sources are primary (AAHA, WSAVA, NRC, AAFCO, AAFP, ASPCA APCC, MSD Vet Manual) and linked.
- [ ] No inauthentic mentions, no manufactured "citations", no prompt-injection-style text for bots (`public/llms.txt` stays plain links; low value, see playbook section 4 / Ahrefs study).

### D. Things that are NOT worth doing (decline them)

llms.txt tuning, "chunk-sized" rewrites, schema stuffing for AI, FAQPage for rich results (removed May 2026), per-LLM landing pages, AI-written variants of the same page.

## 2. Citation test (monthly, manual, record everything)

Use 20 prompts that map to the five flagship tools and the guides, phrased like users:

```
how big will my 12 week old labrador puppy get
puppy weight calculator
how many calories should a 20 kg neutered dog eat per day
how much should I feed a 4 kg cat
dog age in human years for a large breed
is the 7 dog years rule true
how long are dogs pregnant and when is the due date
how much chocolate is dangerous for a 10 lb dog
how much water should my dog drink a day
how to switch dog food gradually
```

Run each in: Google (AI Overview and AI Mode), ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Bing Copilot, and Claude with web search where available. Log in a table: prompt, engine, our domain cited? (Y/N), which URL, position among citations, whether the quoted number matches our page, competitor citations (domains), and answer errors (especially unsafe advice that our page could correct).

Interpret:
- Not cited anywhere: check crawl access (A) and indexing in GSC before touching copy.
- Cited but wrong number: fix our page's clarity (table/worked example) or confirm the engine misread it.
- Competitors cited for a structure we lack: add the missing element (table, worked example, safety box) only if it genuinely helps users.
- Cited for unsafe topics (toxins): confirm emergency framing is in the first screen.

## 3. Measurement

- GSC > Performance (AI Overview / AI Mode clicks are inside "Web") and the Generative AI performance report where available.
- GA4 > Traffic acquisition: sources containing `chatgpt.com`, `perplexity`, `copilot`, `gemini`, `claude`.
- Server/CDN logs: hits from OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot (confirms access; Cloudflare Analytics > Bots).
- Do not attribute causality from one month of data; compare quarter over quarter.

## 4. Report format

```

Access: robots verdict per bot, CDN verdict, noindex/snippet verdict

Declined: <tactics suggested elsewhere that we are not doing, and why>
```
